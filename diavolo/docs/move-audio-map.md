# Move audio assignments

All audio is decoded from the local Steam ASBR installation. Combat PL banks contain voices; the old inventory label `asbr_sfx` is retained for provenance only. These are best-fit assignments, not a claim of complete original event timing. Transcriptions are machine-assisted and may mishear battle cries. No synthetic voice lines or effects are used.
