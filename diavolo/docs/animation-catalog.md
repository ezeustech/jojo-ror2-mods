# Extracted Eyes of Heaven animation catalog

Source: the local mot1/mot2 conversion and Unity import report. This lists all 172 clips currently imported: 115 Diavolo, 57 King Crimson. It is not a claim that these banks contain every cinematic in the entire game.

Full imported names have the form `5dvl01bod1|5dvl01_<clip>` or `5kcr01bod1|5kcr01_<clip>`. Durations are source clip lengths before mod playback speed adjustments.

Mappings distinguish inspected motion, current mod use, and naming-based inference. The special/GMC sequence numbers have not been matched to official EOH move titles. Reusing a clip for impale, barrage, blood throw, or counter in this mod does not establish its original EOH move identity.
